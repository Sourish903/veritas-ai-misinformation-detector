🌐

Any content type

Analyze news articles, social media posts, emails, blogs, or any written claim up to 5,000 characters.

Legitimate

Content that is factually grounded and accurately framed. Claims are supported, context is present, and language is measured.

Misleading

Content that distorts facts through exaggeration, missing context, or selectively framed truths — not outright false, but designed to deceive.

Highly Deceptive

Content designed to deceive. Contains fabricated facts, conspiracy framing, or deliberate manipulation with little basis in verified reality.

🤖

Transformer-based model

Built on a fine-tuned transformer architecture trained on large corpora of labeled misinformation and verified news content. The model evaluates linguistic patterns, claim structure, emotional tone, and known deception signals simultaneously.

📈

Multi-label classification

Rather than a simple binary true/false output, the model produces a probability distribution across the three severity tiers — giving you nuanced results instead of oversimplified verdicts.

💾

Analysis history

Every analysis you run is saved locally to your browser. Your history persists across sessions so you can revisit past results, compare analyses, or reload a previous text with one click.

📤

Export and share

Copy any result as a formatted plain-text summary or export it as a print-ready PDF report — useful for sharing findings or keeping a record of your analysis.
